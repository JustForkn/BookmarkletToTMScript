Turn a bookmarklet into a userscript

Drag a bookmarklet from your bookmarks bar into the box below. It'll pull out the code and wrap it as a Tampermonkey userscript that fires on its own — no click needed.

1 Get the bookmarklet's code

Chrome and Edge deliberately block reading a javascript: link's real contents through drag-and-drop — it's a security measure, not something a page can work around. The reliable way in: open your bookmarks manager, right-click the bookmarklet → Edit, and copy the text from the URL field. Paste it below.
